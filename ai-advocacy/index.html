Copy a prompt below and paste it into the AI of your choice to generate something useful for your union or advocacy work.

Prompt: Member Communication
Write a clear, supportive message to educators about an important school or union issue. Use a professional but approachable tone. Include a short explanation of why the issue matters, one concrete next step people can take, and a closing that encourages collective action.
Prompt: Contract or Policy Summary
I am a union leader. Summarize the most important points from this policy document in plain language educators can understand. Highlight any changes, rights, responsibilities, and potential concerns. Then suggest three questions members might ask.
