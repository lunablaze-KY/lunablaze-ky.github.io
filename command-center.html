How to Use This Space

  1. Choose a tool below based on what you need to monitor, explain, or create.
  2. Copy the prompt and paste it into ChatGPT or another AI tool.
  3. Replace the bracketed text with your state, district, bill, board item, or issue.
  4. Review the output carefully and verify details before sharing publicly.

Why It Matters

Advocacy moves faster when you can catch policy changes early, translate them clearly, and respond with confidence. This page helps turn scattered information into usable action for educators and communities.

  • Track legislation and board action
  • Watch budgets and staffing changes
  • Generate testimony, emails, and talking points
  • Translate dense policy into plain language

Universal Policy Monitor

Track legislation, budgets, board actions, and news

Use this to scan multiple public education signals at once and catch patterns early.

Search for new updates related to public education policy in [STATE, DISTRICT, OR REGION]. Scan the following sources when possible: - state legislature and education committee activity - state department of education releases - school district announcements and budget documents - school board agendas and meeting materials - credible local news coverage Focus on developments involving: - early childhood education (ECE, preschool) - special education and IDEA compliance - multilingual or immersion programs - teacher planning time and workload - staffing levels and educator shortages - class sizes, ratios, and caseload limits - therapist and support staff roles - school funding or budget reductions - curriculum, accountability, or assessment policy - union or labor issues affecting educators Notify me only about meaningful new developments. For each update provide: 1. A short summary of the change 2. The source or policy action 3. Why it matters for educators, students, or public schools 4. Potential advocacy implications

Rapid Response Generator

Turn updates into testimony, emails, and action steps

Use this when a bill drops, a story breaks, or an agenda item surfaces and you need a quick advocacy response.

You are assisting an education advocate responding to a new policy development. Based on the policy update below, generate a rapid advocacy response. Policy Update: [PASTE NEWS, BILL UPDATE, OR BOARD AGENDA ITEM HERE] Create the following: 1. Plain-language summary 2. Key concerns for students, educators, public schools, and equity 3. 4-6 talking points 4. A short public comment statement 5. A concise email to a policymaker 6. A short social media post encouraging constructive engagement

Policy Translator

Convert dense policy language into plain English

Use this to explain bills, board items, and policy documents to educators and families in a clearer way.

You are assisting an educator or community advocate in explaining an education policy update. Policy Text: [PASTE BILL LANGUAGE, POLICY DOCUMENT, OR BOARD AGENDA ITEM] Translate the information into plain language. Provide: 1. A simple summary 2. What the policy changes 3. Why it matters for students, educators, families, and school resources 4. 3-5 key questions people may ask 5. A short explanation suitable for a parent newsletter or school communication

Suggested Workflow

1

Monitor

Use the policy, legislative, or budget prompts to catch important developments early.

2

Translate

Turn dense policy language into clear explanations educators and families can understand.

3

Respond

Generate talking points, testimony, media responses, and policymaker emails quickly.

4

Organize

Share adapted outputs with educators, families, members, and community partners.

AI can help you move faster, but people still provide the judgment, relationships, and courage. Always verify policy details before posting, emailing, or speaking publicly.
