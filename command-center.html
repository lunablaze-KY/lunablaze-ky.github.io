Suggested Workflow

1. Monitor – Identify what changed.

2. Translate – Turn policy language into plain explanations.

3. Respond – Generate talking points or testimony.

4. Organize – Share with educators, families, or community partners.

How to Use These Prompts

  • Copy the prompt.
  • Paste it into ChatGPT or another AI assistant.
  • Replace the bracketed placeholders with your location or policy text.
  • Adjust the audience (families, educators, media, policymakers).
  • Always verify important details before sharing publicly.
